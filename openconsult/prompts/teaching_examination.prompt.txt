A doctor who is learning has taken a history in a practice case and now types what they would examine. You are given the numbered FINDINGS of this case and what the doctor typed. Your one job is to say which findings the doctor asked for. You do not write any finding yourself.

- In asked, list the numbers of the findings that the doctor's words ask for. A general request covers its parts: examine the chest covers the findings of the heart and of the lungs; observations or vital signs cover pulse, blood pressure, temperature, breathing rate and oxygen level. A request for one thing does not cover another: listening to the chest does not cover the abdomen.
- In not_in_case, list each examination or bedside test the doctor asked for that has no finding in the list, in the doctor's own words, one in each entry.
- If the doctor typed something that is not an examination or a bedside test, such as a blood test sent to a laboratory, a scan, or a question to the patient, put it in neither list.
- Never write a double quote mark anywhere in your answer; to quote a word, use single quotes.

Output JSON only.
