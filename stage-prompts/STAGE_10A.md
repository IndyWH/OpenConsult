OpenConsult v2.0, run 10a: the four prompts of teaching mode as files, their jobs in the door, and the bench of teaching on both models, report only

WHAT THIS IS
Stage 10 is teaching mode, typed: a learner picks a topic, takes a history from a patient played by the model, says what they would examine, answers three questions and gets feedback. The owner's picture of it: a virtual actor who is given a patient scenario, animated by the model. A learner who is taught something wrong is harmed (12.3). So before any page stands on the four prompts, both models are put through the same test, with six hard marks set before any run (15.14, ruling 14): the actor never states what its scenario does not hold, never denies what it does hold, never names a diagnosis, a test, a treatment or advice beyond its scenario and never leaves the role; the examination never says nothing is there where the scenario holds a finding; the feedback never credits a red flag that was not asked about and never points to a line that does not exist; a fresh scenario never holds a dose and never gives its diagnosis away. This run puts the owner's words into the prompts folder, adds the four jobs to the door, builds the module that fills their frames, builds the bench, runs it on Gemma 4 on this machine and on Claude Haiku, and reports. IT BUILDS NOTHING A LEARNER CAN SEE (R31): no page, no route, no table, no setting. Done when the owner sees: the bench report, checked by Cowork from the stored replies, and has ruled on it. The pages are run 10b.
The spec is V2_SPEC.md. Read section 12 whole; 10.2; in section 4 the rules R13, R20, R21, R24, R30 and R31; 15.8 on prompts; then 15.14 whole: rulings 1 to 15 and THE DETAILS OF RUN 10A at its end, which are the design of this run and which you build as written. Then, in LOGS, stage10-bench/README.md, whole: it is part of the details. Then, in this repo, stage-prompts/STAGE_06C.md, STAGE_09D.md, STAGE_09D2.md and STAGE_09F.md: EVERY RULE OF THOSE FOUR HOLDS IN THIS RUN except where this prompt changes it. Then, in LOGS, ROOM_CHECKS_6C_TO_6D2.md: its six rules under How we work are rules of this run. Where this prompt and the spec differ, the spec wins: stop and report.
OpenConsult is a research and education prototype. It is not a medical device. Never real patients. Every consultation and every scenario is acted or made up.
