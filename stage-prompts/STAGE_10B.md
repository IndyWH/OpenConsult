OpenConsult v2.0, run 10b: the pages of teaching mode, typed: the teaching page, a case from the patient's first words to the feedback, the bank, the kept cases, the flag, the section Teaching of Settings and the cloud lines

WHAT THIS IS
Stage 10 is teaching mode, typed. The owner's picture of it: a virtual actor who is given a patient scenario, animated by the model, and questioned by the learner. Run 10a put the four prompts into the app and benched them; no learner can see anything yet. This run builds what a learner sees. A learner picks a case, reads who the patient is and what they say first, types questions and is answered by the actor, ends the history, types what they would examine and is shown the scenario's own findings, answers three questions, and gets feedback that is built from the scenario and quotes the learner's own lines.
A LEARNER WHO IS TAUGHT SOMETHING WRONG IS HARMED (12.3). So three things matter above the rest. One: every finding and every word of feedback on a page is the scenario's own, and every quoted line is the learner's own; no page shows a sentence a model wrote as if it were fact. Two: THE KNOWN FAULT (15.14, ruling 20): on the local model the feedback credits a red flag that was not asked about in about one feedback in five, so a learner must be able to check every credit against their own line, and where there is no line the page says so plainly. Three: nothing of the scenario beyond what the patient has said reaches a page before the feedback, the name of the diagnosis least of all.
Done when the owner sees: he works through one case by typing (15.4, row 10).
The spec is V2_SPEC.md. Read section 12 whole; 10.4; 5.1 and 5.2; in section 4 the rules R4, R13, R15, R21, R22, R24, R30 and R31; in section 16 the decisions D5, D6, D9, D10 and D36; in 15.13 rulings 5, 8, 15, 48 and 55 and the first fenced text; then 15.14 whole: rulings 1 to 23 and THE DETAILS OF RUN 10B at its end, which are the design of this run and which you build as written. Then, in this repo, stage-prompts/STAGE_06C.md, STAGE_09B.md, STAGE_09C.md, STAGE_09F.md and STAGE_10A.md: EVERY RULE OF THOSE FIVE HOLDS IN THIS RUN except where this prompt changes it. Where this prompt and the spec differ, the spec wins: stop and report.
OpenConsult is a research and education prototype. It is not a medical device. Never real patients. Every consultation and every scenario is acted or made up.
