WHAT THIS IS
Stage 5 is about speech, built in five steps. This is 5e, the last. On 21 Oct 2026 the owner and a second reader recorded three acted consultations (scripts 19, 15 and 14) on a laptop's own microphone. Until now every figure of the speech bench was a rehearsal on old recordings and machine voices, and no figure counted. In this run the figures count. From the patient's side it asks: which speech choice loses or changes the least of what was said, on real voices, all the way to the note; which of the two cloud services a laptop user should be offered first; and whether a service is fast enough for the app's own questioner, which has 3 seconds from the patient's last word. Three things are new beside the bench of 5c: each cloud service also gets each recording whole, once, as it would at Stop, and the bench deletes it there at once and proves it gone; a line that says how soon the patient's last sentence is final; and the doctor's voice cut from the recordings themselves for the voice method. The run opens with one small commit to the app: the live page asks the browser for sound at 48,000 samples a second. Nothing else of the app changes, and nothing of the bench is built into the app. Done when the owner sees: the report's first lines, checked by Cowork from the raw files, and has chosen the defaults.
The spec is V2_SPEC.md. Stage 5 is section 15.9. Read rulings 1 to 33 there, then the details of the stage, of 5a, of 5b, of part one and part two of 5c, and then the details of 5e, which this run builds; then section 13, and in 15.13 rulings 1, 9 and 23. Then, in this repo, stage-prompts/STAGE_05C1.md and STAGE_05C2.md: EVERY RULE OF THOSE TWO PROMPTS HOLDS IN THIS RUN except where this prompt changes it. Then STAGE_09A.md for two things only: the rule of the KEY folder and the use of the owner's engine script. Then, in LOGS, STAGE5C1_REPORT.md and STAGE5C2_REPORT.md: their last sections and what was fixed after Cowork's checks. Where this prompt and the spec differ, the spec wins: stop and report.
OpenConsult is a research and education prototype. Never real patients. Every consultation is acted.
